OVERVIEW

What Is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry encompasses aesthetic procedures designed to enhance the color, shape, size, alignment, and overall facial harmony of your natural smile.

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin, custom ceramic shells bonded to the front of teeth for flawless aesthetics.

Laser Teeth Whitening

Instant 45-minute in-office whitening that brightens enamel up to 8 shades lighter.

Composite Composite Bonding

Artistic composite sculpting to repair small chips, gaps, and uneven enamel edges.

Who Can Benefit From Cosmetic Dentistry?

Severely discoloured, yellowed, or fluorosis-stained teeth

POST-TREATMENT CARE

Care & Aftercare Guidelines

Follow these simple post-treatment instructions to ensure swift recovery and long-lasting results.

Routine Daily Hygiene

Brush twice daily and floss between veneers/bonded teeth to maintain healthy gums.

Avoid Hard Biting Forces

Do not bite hard objects (ice, hard candies, pen caps) directly with front cosmetic veneers.

Nightguard Protection

Wear a custom nightguard if you grind your teeth at night to safeguard porcelain edges.

Professional Cleaning

Schedule routine 6-month checkups and gentle polishing at Pepper Dent.
