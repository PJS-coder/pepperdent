OVERVIEW

What Is Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment involves removing infected or inflamed pulp tissue from inside the tooth, thoroughly sterilizing the inner root canals, and sealing them with biocompatible gutta-percha.

Pulp Debridement

Careful removal of infected nerve tissue, blood vessels, and bacteria from inner chambers.

Rotary Canal Shaping

Disinfecting and shaping complex root canals using flexible rotary endodontic files.

Hermetic Sealing & Crown

Filling sterile canals with gutta-percha followed by a protective custom crown.

CANDIDACY

Who Needs Root Canal Therapy?

Severe, throbbing tooth pain while chewing or biting down
Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold food and drinks
Swelling or small pimple-like bumps on the gums near a painful tooth
Deep tooth decay that has reached the inner dental pulp
Chipped, cracked, or traumatized tooth with nerve exposure
RISKS & CONCERNS

Risks of Delaying Root Canal Treatment

Spread of bacterial infection to surrounding jawbone tissue
Painful abscess formation causing severe facial swelling
Irreversible nerve death leading to inevitable tooth extraction
Significantly higher costs associated with tooth replacement
Chronic infection spreading to neighboring healthy teeth
STEP-BY-STEP

Treatment Journey & Process

Our structured 5-step approach ensures safety, comfort, and precise results.

01

Digital X-Ray Examination

High-resolution digital X-rays to assess root anatomy and depth of pulp infection.

02

Gentle Local Anesthesia

Complete numbing of the target tooth and surrounding gums for painless care.

03

Pulp Removal & Disinfection

Removing infected pulp and thoroughly irrigating canals with antibacterial agents.

04

Canal Sealing (Gutta-Percha)

Filling sterile root canals with durable biocompatible gutta-percha sealing material.

05

Protective Crown Fitting

Placing a custom Zirconia or ceramic crown to restore full chewing strength.

POST-TREATMENT CARE

Care & Aftercare Guidelines

Follow these simple post-treatment instructions to ensure swift recovery and long-lasting results.

Temporary Chewing Precautions

Avoid chewing hard or sticky foods on the treated side until your permanent crown is fitted.

Maintain Daily Hygiene

Brush twice daily and floss gently around the root-canal-treated tooth.

Take Prescribed Meds

Take mild analgesics or antibiotics as advised by your endodontist for complete comfort.

Crown Placement Recall

Attend your scheduled follow-up appointment for final permanent crown cementation.
