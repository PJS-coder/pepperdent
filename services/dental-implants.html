OVERVIEW

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a permanent, multi-part tooth restoration engineered to mimic the function, strength, and aesthetics of a natural tooth from root to crown.

Implant Fixture

A medical-grade titanium post placed into the jawbone that fuses with natural bone tissue.

Precision Abutment

The durable connector component attached to the implant post to anchor the final crown.

Custom Ceramic Crown

A tooth-colored Zirconia or E-Max porcelain crown custom-crafted to blend with your smile.

CANDIDACY

Who Needs Dental Implants?

Patients with one or more missing natural teeth
Severely broken or decayed teeth that require extraction
Individuals seeking a permanent alternative to loose removable dentures
Patients wanting to preserve jawbone density and facial contours
Desire to chew firm foods with full strength and stability
RISKS & CONCERNS

Consequences of Leaving Missing Teeth Untreated

Irreversible jawbone loss (resorption) leading to facial sagging
Adjacent healthy teeth shifting, tilting, and loosening into empty gaps
Impaired chewing efficiency and digestive discomfort
Uneven bite contact causing jaw joint pain and TMJ headaches
Loss of self-esteem and hesitation when speaking or smiling
STEP-BY-STEP

Treatment Journey & Process

Our structured 5-step approach ensures safety, comfort, and precise results.

01

Initial Assessment & 3D Imaging

Comprehensive digital oral examination and 3D CBCT scans to evaluate jawbone density.

02

Implant Surgery Placement

Painless surgical insertion of the titanium implant fixture under local anesthesia.

03

Osseointegration (Healing)

A 3-6 month healing phase where the jawbone securely fuses around the implant post.

04

Abutment Attachment

Attaching the custom connector abutment once full bone integration is complete.

05

Final Crown Placement

Securing your custom Zirconia or ceramic crown for immediate natural function and beauty.

OPTIONS

Types of Implant Options Offered

Single Tooth Implants

Replaces an individual missing tooth without altering adjacent healthy teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Restores multiple adjacent missing teeth using 2 or more implants for maximum stability.

All-on-4 / All-on-6 Full Rehab

Comprehensive fixed tooth replacement for an entire arch using 4 or 6 strategic implants.

POST-TREATMENT CARE

Care & Aftercare Guidelines

Follow these simple post-treatment instructions to ensure swift recovery and long-lasting results.

Brush & Floss Daily

Maintain meticulous daily brushing and interdental flossing around implant crowns.

Avoid Biting Ice & Bones

Do not chew hard items like ice or hard candies directly on new ceramic crowns.

Tobacco Abstinence

Refrain from smoking as tobacco impairs bone healing and implant fusion.

6-Month Dental Checks

Attend routine 6-month checkups and professional cleanings to ensure long-term stability.
