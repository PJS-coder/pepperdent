OVERVIEW

What Is Pediatric Dentistry?

Pediatric dentistry specializes in managing the growth, development, cavity prevention, and treatment of primary (baby) and young permanent teeth.

Dental Sealants

Protective thin coatings applied to chewing surfaces of molars to seal out cavities.

Fluoride Therapy

Enamel-strengthening topical fluoride treatment to protect young teeth against decay.

Space Maintainers

Custom appliances used when a baby tooth is lost early to reserve space for adult teeth.

RISKS & CONCERNS

Why Primary (Baby) Teeth Matter

Baby teeth hold crucial space for the proper alignment of adult permanent teeth
Untreated cavities cause severe pain, infections, and missed school days
Healthy teeth allow proper chewing and nutritional absorption
Essential for clear speech development and pronunciation
Early positive dental visits prevent lifelong dental anxiety

POST-TREATMENT CARE

Care & Aftercare Guidelines

Follow these simple post-treatment instructions to ensure swift recovery and long-lasting results.

Supervised Brushing

Supervise brushing twice daily with a pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste.

Limit Sugary Snacks

Limit sticky candies, fruit juices, and bedtime sugary drinks to prevent cavities.

Early First Dental Visit

Schedule your child’s first dental visit by their 1st birthday or first tooth.

Sports Mouthguards

Use custom sports mouthguards during school sports and outdoor physical activities.
