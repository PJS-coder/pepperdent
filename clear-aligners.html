OVERVIEW

What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are transparent, medical-grade thermoplastic trays engineered from 3D digital impressions to apply gentle, continuous forces that align teeth comfortably.

3D Digital Scan

Precise digital optical scan of your teeth without messy traditional impression materials.

Custom Aligner Series

A sequence of custom-molded clear trays changed every 1 to 2 weeks.

Smart Attachments

Tiny enamel-colored composite buttons for complex tooth rotation control.

CANDIDACY

Who Is a Candidate for Clear Aligners?

Dental implants may be considered for patients who have:

Adults and teens seeking a discreet, nearly invisible straightening solution
Mild to moderate crowding, overlapping, or gaps between teeth
Patients who want to eat without food restrictions during treatment
Busy professionals preferring fewer in-clinic adjustment visits
Relapse cases after past braces treatment

Your dentist will examine your teeth, gums and overall oral health before recommending implant treatment.

PATIENT GUIDANCE

Aftercare Following Dental Implant Treatment

Following your dentist’s instructions after implant treatment is important for proper healing. Depending on your treatment, you may be advised to:

Wear aligner trays for 20-22 hours daily for optimal movement
Rinse trays with lukewarm water and clean daily with a soft toothbrush
Remove trays when consuming anything other than plain cool water
Store aligners safely in their protective case when not in use
Wear prescribed clear retainers at night after completing your aligner series

Your dentist will provide personalised aftercare guidance based on your procedure.
