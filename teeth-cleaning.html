OVERVIEW

What Is Dental Scaling & Polishing?

Dental scaling is a routine preventative procedure using high-frequency ultrasonic vibrations to remove hardened tartar and bacterial plaque from teeth and below the gumline.

Ultrasonic Scaler

High-speed micro-vibrational tip that gently detaches hardened calculus without scraping enamel.

Subgingival Cleaning

Clearing bacterial plaque from shallow gum pockets to eliminate gingivitis.

Prophylaxis Polishing

Smoothing enamel surfaces with fluoride paste to remove surface stains and prevent plaque regrowth.

CANDIDACY

Who Needs Teeth Cleaning & Scaling?

Dental implants may be considered for patients who have:

Bleeding or swollen gums during brushing or flossing
Persistent bad breath (halitosis) or unpleasant taste in the mouth
Visible yellow, brown, or black tartar buildup near the gumline
Routine preventive care recommended every 6 months for all adults
Smokers, tea/coffee drinkers, and diabetic patients requiring gum care

Your dentist will examine your teeth, gums and overall oral health before recommending implant treatment.

ORAL HEALTH IMPACT

Consequences of Neglecting Dental Cleaning

Missing teeth can affect more than just appearance. They may also lead to:

Progression from reversible gingivitis to destructive periodontitis
Receding gums, deep pocket formation, and jawbone loss
Loosening of teeth and eventual tooth loss
Chronic bad breath and unpleasant oral odor
Systemic health links to cardiovascular conditions and unmanaged diabetes

Replacing missing teeth can help restore normal oral function and improve your smile.

PATIENT GUIDANCE

Aftercare Following Dental Implant Treatment

Following your dentist’s instructions after implant treatment is important for proper healing. Depending on your treatment, you may be advised to:

Brush teeth twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste
Floss interdental spaces daily to prevent plaque accumulation between teeth
Rinse with an antimicrobial mouthwash as recommended by your dentist
Limit frequent snacking on sugary or acidic foods
Schedule your next professional cleaning and checkup in 6 months

Your dentist will provide personalised aftercare guidance based on your procedure.
