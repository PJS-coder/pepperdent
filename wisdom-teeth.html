OVERVIEW

What Is Wisdom Tooth Extraction?

Wisdom tooth extraction is a minor oral surgical procedure to remove the third molars at the back of the mouth when they are impacted, misaligned, decay-prone, or causing pain.

Diagnostic X-Ray / OPG

Full-mouth panoramic digital X-rays to assess nerve location and root curvature.

Painless Anesthesia

Targeted local numbing to ensure a completely painless surgical experience.

Gentle Tooth Removal

Precise, atraumatic tooth sectioning and removal preserving surrounding bone.

CANDIDACY

Signs You May Need Wisdom Tooth Removal

Severe pain, throbbing, or pressure at the back of your jaw
Swollen, tender, or bleeding gums around partially erupted wisdom teeth (pericoronitis)
Difficulty opening your mouth or swallowing comfortably
Wisdom teeth pushing against adjacent molars causing crowding or decay
Formation of cysts or decay in hard-to-clean third molars
RISKS & CONCERNS

Complications of Ignoring Impacted Wisdom Teeth

Irreversible decay on second molars due to food trapping
Recurrent painful gum infections and facial swelling (pericoronitis)
Crowding and misalignment of front teeth from forward pressure
Cyst or tumor development in the jawbone surrounding impacted teeth
Severe jaw joint pain and difficulty chewing

POST-TREATMENT CARE

Care & Aftercare Guidelines

Follow these simple post-treatment instructions to ensure swift recovery and long-lasting results.

Gauze Pressure

Bite gently on the gauze pack for 45-60 minutes post-procedure to stop bleeding.

Ice Pack Compression

Apply an ice pack to the outside of your cheek for 15-minute intervals during the first 24h.

Soft Diet Selection

Eat soft, cool foods (yogurt, ice cream, smoothies, soup) for the first 2-3 days.

Avoid Straws & Smoking

Avoid straws, spitting forcefully, or smoking for 72h to prevent dry socket formation.
