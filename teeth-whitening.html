OVERVIEW

What Is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening is a non-invasive cosmetic dental procedure that uses dentist-grade peroxide gel activated by LED/laser light to break down organic enamel stains.

Gingival Barrier Protection

Protective light-cured resin barrier applied to safeguard gums during whitening.

Medical Whitening Gel

High-potency hydrogen/carbamide peroxide formula for deep stain oxidation.

LED Activation Light

Specialized cold LED lamp that accelerates breakdown of stubborn discoloration.

PATIENT GUIDANCE

Aftercare Following Dental Implant Treatment

Following your dentist’s instructions after implant treatment is important for proper healing. Depending on your treatment, you may be advised to:

Follow a "White Diet" (avoid coffee, tea, soy sauce, red wine, turmeric) for 48 hours
Refrain from smoking or tobacco products to prevent rapid restaining
Use a desensitizing toothpaste if temporary mild cold sensitivity occurs
Maintain meticulous daily brushing and flossing routines
Schedule regular 6-month dental cleanings to maintain your bright shade

Your dentist will provide personalised aftercare guidance based on your procedure.
