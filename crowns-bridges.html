OVERVIEW

What Are Crowns & Bridges?

A dental crown is a custom-molded protective cap that fits over a weak or damaged tooth, while a dental bridge replaces missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent natural teeth or implants.

Full-Coverage Crown Cap

Custom ceramic cap engineered to restore tooth shape, strength, and appearance.

Abutment Support Teeth

Prepared natural teeth on either side of a gap that support a dental bridge.

Pontic Replacement Tooth

The solid ceramic artificial tooth suspended between bridge crowns to fill a gap.

CANDIDACY

Who Needs Crowns & Bridges?

Teeth weakened by large cavities or root canal treatment
Cracked, broken, or severely worn natural teeth
One or more missing teeth requiring fixed bridge replacement
Discoloured or misshapen teeth needing aesthetic restoration
Restoration over dental implants
RISKS & CONCERNS

Risks of Leaving Damaged or Missing Teeth Unprotected

High risk of tooth fracture requiring full extraction
Shifting and tilting of neighboring teeth into empty spaces
Uneven bite contact causing jaw joint pain and TMJ strain
Chewing difficulties and food impaction leading to gum disease
Premature aging appearance due to collapse of facial height
STEP-BY-STEP

Treatment Journey & Process

Our structured 5-step approach ensures safety, comfort, and precise results.

01

Initial Exam & Shade Selection

Detailed digital assessment and exact tooth shade matching using VITA shade guides.

02

Tooth Preparation

Gentle reshaping of target teeth under local anesthesia to create space for crowns.

03

Digital 3D Impression

Scanning precise 3D digital impressions for CAD/CAM ceramic milling.

04

Temporary Crown Fitting

Fitting a comfortable temporary crown while your permanent Zirconia crown is crafted.

05

Permanent Cementation

Verifying fit, bite, and aesthetics before permanent medical bonding of your custom crown.

POST-TREATMENT CARE

Care & Aftercare Guidelines

Follow these simple post-treatment instructions to ensure swift recovery and long-lasting results.

Daily Crown Margins Cleaning

Brush twice daily and floss around crown margins every night.

Interdental & Floss Threaders

Use threader floss or interdental brushes to keep bridge undersides clear of food particles.

Protect Against Night Grinding

Wear a custom nightguard if you suffer from teeth grinding (bruxism) to protect ceramic enamel.

Bi-Annual Polish & Checkup

Visit Pepper Dent every 6 months for routine crown checkups and professional polishing.
