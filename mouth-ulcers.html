OVERVIEW

What Are Mouth Ulcers & TMJ Disorders?

Mouth ulcers are painful sores inside the oral cavity, while TMJ disorders affect the temporomandibular joint, causing jaw clicking, facial pain, and restricted mouth opening.

Topical Pain Barrier

Soothing protective mucosal gels that shield open sores from acidic foods and friction.

Biostimulation Laser

Low-level laser therapy (LLLT) that instantly neutralizes ulcer pain and speeds up healing.

TMJ Nightguards

Custom jaw alignment splints that relieve pressure on TMJ muscles during sleep.

CANDIDACY

Who Needs Mouth Ulcer & TMJ Treatment?

Recurrent painful aphthous ulcers lasting more than 7-10 days
Clicking, popping, or locking sensations in the jaw joint
Facial pain or stiffness when chewing or waking up in the morning
White or red patches inside cheeks, tongue, or lips requiring evaluation
Chronic cheek biting or sharp tooth irritation causing sores
RISKS & CONCERNS

Risks of Neglecting Persistent Mouth Sores or TMJ Pain

Secondary bacterial or fungal infection of open oral ulcers
Chronic TMJ joint degeneration and permanent chewing difficulty
Undiagnosed oral precancerous lesions left unmonitored
Severe nutritional deficiency due to painful eating
Chronic tension headaches and neck soreness
