OVERVIEW

What Is Periodontal Gum Treatment?

Periodontal treatment encompasses therapeutic deep scaling, root planing, laser biostimulation, and gum surgery designed to eliminate deep bacterial infection beneath the gumline.

Deep Root Planing

Smoothing infected root surfaces to remove deep bacterial toxins and calculus.

Laser Periodontics

Sterilizing deep periodontal pockets and promoting firm gum reattachment.

Local Antibiotic Therapy

Targeted placement of antimicrobial gel directly into deep gum pockets.

CANDIDACY

Who Needs Advanced Gum Treatment?

Dental implants may be considered for patients who have:

Gums that bleed easily when brushing, flossing, or eating
Swollen, red, tender, or receding gumlines exposing root surfaces
Persistent bad breath (halitosis) that does not go away with mouthwash
Teeth feeling loose, shifting position, or changes in bite fit
Deep pocket depths (4mm+) measured around teeth during checkups

Your dentist will examine your teeth, gums and overall oral health before recommending implant treatment.

ORAL HEALTH IMPACT

Health Dangers of Untreated Periodontitis

Missing teeth can affect more than just appearance. They may also lead to:

Irreversible destruction of jawbone tissue supporting natural teeth
Progressive tooth mobility leading to premature tooth loss
Increased risk of systemic inflammation affecting heart health and diabetes
Severe gum recession exposing sensitive root surfaces
Recurrent painful periodontal abscesses

Replacing missing teeth can help restore normal oral function and improve your smile.

Dental Implant Treatment Process

A structured 5-step journey to restoring your missing teeth with precision care.

PATIENT GUIDANCE

Aftercare Following Dental Implant Treatment

Following your dentist’s instructions after implant treatment is important for proper healing. Depending on your treatment, you may be advised to:

Brush gently twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and interdental brushes
Rinse daily with prescribed chlorhexidine or botanical antimicrobial mouthwash
Floss interdental contacts daily to prevent bacterial plaque accumulation
Avoid tobacco smoking which severely restricts gum blood supply and healing
Schedule 3 to 4 month periodontal maintenance checkups at Pepper Dent

Your dentist will provide personalised aftercare guidance based on your procedure.
